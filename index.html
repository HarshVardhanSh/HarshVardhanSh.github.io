Selected work

Recent papers.

These papers represent my current work across causal inference, counterfactual reasoning, and representation learning for long-form video understanding.

UAI 2026 · Accepted · First author

Fixed-Confidence Best-Arm Identification for Causal Mediation Analysis

Harsh Shrivastava, Yuta Kawakami, Junpei Komiyama, and Jin Tian.

We formulate maximization of the expected natural direct potential outcome as a fixed-confidence best-arm identification problem, establish identification from interventional distributions, derive instance-dependent sample-complexity lower bounds, and develop an asymptotically optimal Track-and-Stop algorithm.

Causal mediation
Under review · Submitted to AAAI 2027

Evaluating Joint Counterfactual Functionals from Marginal Distributions and Edgewise Correlations

Naoya Hashimoto, Harsh Shrivastava, Yuta Kawakami, and Jin Tian.

We study how partial dependence information can constrain joint counterfactual quantities when the complete joint counterfactual distribution is unavailable.

ECCV 2026 · To appear

MoVA: Learning Asymmetric Dual Projections for Modular Long Video-Text Alignment

Peiyuan Zhu, Shaoan Xie, Zijian Li, Yifan Shen, Namrata Deka, Harsh Shrivastava, Guangyi Chen, and Kun Zhang.

This work develops asymmetric dual projections for modular alignment between long-form video representations and text.
