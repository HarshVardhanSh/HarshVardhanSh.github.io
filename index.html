Current research at MBZUAI

Structured world models

Object-Centric Sparse Transition Representations for World Models

Advisor: Prof. Kun Zhang

Developing object-centric latent representations with sparse transition dependencies for structured world models, with the goal of improving the modularity and generalization of learned dynamics.

Causal mediation and adaptive allocation

Fixed-Confidence Best-Arm Identification for Causal Mediation Analysis

Advisors: Prof. Jin Tian and Prof. Junpei Komiyama
Mentor: Dr. Yuta Kawakami

Formulated maximization of the expected natural direct potential outcome as a fixed-confidence best-arm identification problem. Established identification from interventional distributions, derived instance-dependent sample-complexity lower bounds, and developed an asymptotically optimal Track-and-Stop algorithm for adaptive treatment allocation.

Joint counterfactual evaluation

Evaluating Joint Counterfactual Functionals from Marginal Distributions and Edgewise Correlations

Advisor: Prof. Jin Tian
Collaborators: Naoya Hashimoto and Dr. Yuta Kawakami

Studying how joint counterfactual functionals can be evaluated when only marginal distributions and edgewise correlations are available, without access to the full joint counterfactual distribution. The work uses partial dependence information to constrain otherwise unidentified counterfactual quantities.
